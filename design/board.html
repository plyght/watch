01

Directions

classic silhouette, one loud feature

A · Impact in progress

Chamber case, twisted hex bezel, spider-web crack engraved from an off-centre point, gold emblem over a small balance aperture.

Verdict: the material mix is right. But the dial reads as damage, the crack field fights the feature, the gold emblem is the logo stuck on, and the hex bezel echoes Derschutze's rope.

B · Tumbler alternative

Safe-door diver. The bezel is engraved like a combination dial, and a seconds-driven brass wheel passes under a fixed fence window.

Loud feature: once a minute the gate lines up: unlocked. It moves and it's charming, but it's literal security merch with no semicentric DNA.

C · Breach recommended

The dial plate is torn open from inside, right over the beating balance at 7. Five petals fold outward in the emblem's geometry, and the long blade becomes the 2 o'clock index.

Loud feature: a sculpted brass-edged burst with the live balance in its heart. The emblem is formed, never printed.

04

Bracelet: Chamber band, re-engraved

Same three-row construction, plain outer links, raised centre plaques (8.5 wide, +0.4). Only the engraving changes: scroll filigree becomes shard filigree. Each motif radiates from an off-centre seed point, and plaques alternate mirrored. Relief 0.3, recesses blackened, high points worn bright. Pitch 8.0, thickness 3.8, tapering 20 → 18. The wordmark sits only inside the clasp blade.

05

Logo discipline

may appear
  • Emblem as geometry: the breach petals
  • Off-centre seed logic in bracelet filigree
  • Wordmark engraved on the caseback ring
  • Wordmark inside the clasp blade
may not
  • Printed or applied emblem, anywhere
  • Wordmark on dial, bezel, flanks, lugs
  • Logo on the crown
  • Red seconds or any second brand colour

The story already is the logo. Printing it next to the breach would explain the joke.
